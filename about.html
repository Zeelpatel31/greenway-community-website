About Greenway

Working together to protect nature and strengthen our community.

Our Story

Greenway Community Trust was founded in 2008 by local residents concerned about the decline of green spaces and biodiversity. Since then, the Trust has brought people together to protect and restore the local environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect local green spaces, restore biodiversity and encourage everyone to take part in environmental action.

Our Values

Community

We encourage residents to work together and improve local spaces.

Accessibility

We welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Environmental Responsibility

We support practical activities that protect biodiversity.

Meet Our Team

Helen Marsh coordinates the Trust, supported by five local trustees who contribute their time and experience as volunteer leaders.
