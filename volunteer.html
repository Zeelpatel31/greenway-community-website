Become a Volunteer

Small actions can make a big difference to our environment.

Everyone Is Welcome

No previous experience is needed. Greenway provides equipment, training and refreshments at every event.

What to Expect

Session Length

Most volunteering sessions last between 2 and 4 hours.

Training and Safety

Each session starts with a safety briefing and has a team leader.

Flexible Commitment

You can try one event without making an ongoing commitment.

Before You Attend

Wear sturdy shoes and weather-appropriate clothing. Volunteers under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

Express Your Interest

Complete the form below. Your email application will open with your enquiry ready to send.
